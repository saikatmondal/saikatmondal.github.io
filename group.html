Join the group

I am recruiting MSc students (thesis-based) and undergraduate researchers at Lakehead University, in Thunder Bay. Funded opportunities may be available for strong candidates.

What you might work on

LLM-based code generation and repair that stays faithful to developer intent, explainable and evidence-grounded AI assistance, reliable AI agents with human oversight, and empirical studies of how developers use AI tools.

Who thrives here

Curious people with solid programming skills, interest in software engineering and machine learning, and a habit of asking why. Experience with Python, LLMs, or empirical methods is a plus, not a requirement.

  1. Read a few of my papers and think about which questions excite you. Browse publications.
  2. Email me at with the subject [Prospective MSc] Your Name. Include your CV, transcripts, and a short paragraph on the research direction you would like to pursue.
  3. Apply to the program through Lakehead's MSc in Computer Science and name me as a potential supervisor.

I read every message, but I may not be able to reply individually to every inquiry. Specific, thoughtful emails stand out.
